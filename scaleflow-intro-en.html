CASE 04

Developer with an agency arm · Almaty

30+ contracts a month · deal document flow
Problem

The moment of signing: the client reads the equity-participation contract and finds a stranger's national ID number — a whole other person's, carried over from the previous contract by copying. The lawyer assembled the document by copying the last file, because that's faster than re-typing 12 digits from a crookedly-shot scan. At 30+ contracts a month this roulette went off regularly.

Solution
  • A WhatsApp bot receives scans; on a crooked shot it asks for a re-take with prompts
  • OCR extraction of the ID number, full name, addresses and bank details — no manual re-typing
  • Auto-assembly of the package (contract, act, annexes) from the developer's own templates
  • Reconciliation screen "scan on the left, fields on the right": without the lawyer's confirmation, nothing goes to print

PACKAGE 01

"No lead is lost"

boutique agency · 5–6 brokers · Property Finder + Meta
Before

The agency generated a steady flow of leads from Property Finder, Bayut and Meta Ads, but the biggest problem wasn't traffic — it was response time. When brokers were on viewings, driving between appointments or speaking with other clients, new enquiries waited unanswered. Some leads were replied to within minutes, others much later — and by then many buyers had already spoken to competing agencies. Lead distribution was manual, follow-ups were inconsistent, and management had no clear visibility into who replied first, which enquiries were missed, or where deals were being lost.

What we built
  • An AI assistant in WhatsApp replies to every Property Finder, Bayut and Meta enquiry in seconds — even while brokers are on viewings or driving
  • Automatic lead capture and distribution into the CRM: no more manual routing, every enquiry logged with its source
  • Instant qualification (budget, area, timeline, cash / mortgage) so the broker picks up a warm, ready conversation
  • An owner's dashboard: who replied first, which enquiries were missed, where deals stall
