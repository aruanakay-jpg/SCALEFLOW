01About the company

We build AI automation systems for real estate agencies. We help them respond to leads faster, stop losing inquiries, qualify buyers and tenants automatically, automate WhatsApp, CRM and follow-up, book more viewings, reduce manual work, and improve conversion from lead to appointment.

We don't sell “AI” as technology. We sell business outcomes: faster response time, more qualified leads, more booked viewings, fewer lost clients, less admin, a better sales process.

AI Lead Response & Automation System

15Discovery questions

Ask these once a prospect shows interest.

  1. How do you currently receive most of your leads?
  2. Do leads come from Bayut, Property Finder, Instagram, WhatsApp, website or ads?
  3. How quickly does your team usually respond to new leads?
  4. Do you currently use a CRM?
  5. How many leads do you receive per week?
  6. What happens when a client messages after working hours?
  7. Do you have someone following up with old leads?
  8. Do agents spend a lot of time answering repetitive questions?
  9. Do you currently track response time and conversion?
  10. Would it be valuable if AI could qualify leads before agents speak to them?
  11. Would more booked viewings help your business?
  12. What is the biggest challenge in your current sales process?

16Common objections

We already have agents replying.
That's great. AI isn't here to replace your agents — it helps them respond instantly, qualify leads first, and avoid wasting time on repetitive conversations.
We already use a CRM.
Perfect. Our system can work with your CRM and improve how leads are captured, qualified and followed up.
We don't need AI.
I understand — many agencies feel the same at first. Usually the issue isn't AI itself. It's delayed responses, lost leads and inconsistent follow-up. We help fix that process.
Send me information.
Of course. I can send a short overview — it's also easier to show you in 15 minutes how it could work for your agency. Would tomorrow or Thursday work better?
How much does it cost?
Pricing depends on the setup, number of channels, integrations and complexity. First we do a short consultation to understand your lead process, then recommend the best setup.
We're not interested.
No problem at all. Just so I understand — is it because you already have a system for lead response and follow-up, or is this simply not a priority right now?

17Qualified appointment rules

An appointment is qualified only if all of the following are true:

  • The company is a real estate business
  • The person is a decision maker (or close to it)
  • They understand we offer AI automation
  • They showed interest
  • They agreed to a specific call time
  • The meeting is added to the calendar
  • The lead is added to Notion
  • Notes are complete
Not qualified

A random reply.

A vague “maybe later.”

Qualified

Clear date, time and contact person — booked on the calendar.

23Important sales rules

Don't

Sell technology. Don't say “We build AI.”

Explain every service at once.

Argue with clients.

Promise guaranteed revenue.

Do

Sell outcomes: “We help agencies respond faster, stop losing leads and book more viewings.”

Start with the main problem: lead response, qualification, booked appointments.

Ask questions and understand their process.

Explain results depend on their process, lead volume and implementation.

Say it in simple words

We help real estate agencies stop losing leads. When someone messages the agency, AI replies instantly, qualifies the person, collects their requirements and helps book the next step — so agents focus on serious clients instead of repetitive messages.
