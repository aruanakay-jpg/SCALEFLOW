1. Who we are

SCALEFLOW OS LTD ("ScaleFlow", "we", "us") is a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales under company number 17348735, with its registered office at [REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS].

We build revenue infrastructure for real estate agencies operating in Dubai. For the personal data described in section 2 we are the controller. For personal data held inside a client's own systems we act as a processor — see section 9.

Anything in this policy: privacy@scaleflow.online

2. What we collect

Visitors to this site

  • Pages viewed, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type
  • Campaign parameters (UTM) if you arrived from an advert or a shared link

People who take the Revenue Leak Assessment

  • Name, business email, WhatsApp number, agency name and website
  • Your answers to the assessment questions and the score calculated from them
  • The reference code generated for your submission

People who book a Revenue Audit

  • Name, email, phone number if you provide one, chosen time and time zone
  • Anything you write in the booking notes

Correspondence and clients

  • Emails, WhatsApp messages, and call recordings where you have been told the call is recorded
  • For clients: contact details of your staff, contract and billing records

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To produce your assessment result and run the audit you booked — performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before entering one
  • To contact you about your submission — our legitimate interest in responding to a business enquiry you initiated
  • To improve the assessment and our services, on an aggregated basis — legitimate interests
  • To meet accounting, tax and legal obligations — legal obligation
  • To send marketing communications — consent, which you can withdraw at any time

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your submission, your database or your conversations to train shared or third-party AI models.

4. Who else processes your data

We use the following processors, each bound by a written data processing agreement and permitted to act only on our instructions:

  • Make.com — workflow automation between our systems
  • Bitrix24 — our CRM
  • Cal.com — scheduling of audits
  • Google — Workspace, Calendar and Meet
  • Notion — internal records
  • Meta / WhatsApp — where you choose to contact us on that channel
  • Our website hosting provider

We also disclose data where we are legally required to, and to professional advisers under a duty of confidentiality.

5. International transfers

We are established in the United Kingdom. Most people who use this site are in the United Arab Emirates. Personal data is therefore transferred across borders in the ordinary course of providing our services.

Where personal data leaves the UK we rely on UK adequacy regulations where they exist, and otherwise on the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, together with a transfer risk assessment.

Where personal data relating to individuals in the UAE is processed outside the UAE, we do so on the basis of the contract with you and your consent, and apply the same security standards described in section 8.

6. How long we keep it

  • Assessment submissions — 24 months from the date of submission
  • Bookings and correspondence — 24 months from our last contact with you
  • Client contract and billing records — 6 years, as required by UK company and tax law
  • Marketing consent records — for as long as consent is relied on, plus 12 months

You may ask us to delete your data sooner. See section 7.

7. Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, receive a portable copy, and withdraw consent at any time. If you are in the UAE, the Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on Personal Data Protection gives you equivalent rights.

To exercise any of them, write to privacy@scaleflow.online. We respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or, in the UAE, to the UAE Data Office.

8. How we protect it

  • Isolated environments per client, with encryption in transit and at rest
  • Access limited to the people who need it, under individual accounts with multi-factor authentication
  • No client database is used to train shared models
  • Processors are selected and reviewed for their own security posture

9. Client data — where we act as a processor

When we build infrastructure inside your CRM, your portal accounts and your WhatsApp, we necessarily process personal data about your buyers, tenants and landlords. In that relationship you are the controller and we are the processor.

We act only on your documented instructions, keep your environment isolated from every other client, never use your database for any purpose of our own, and return or securely delete it at the end of the engagement. A data processing agreement is signed as part of every engagement and is available for review before you commit to anything.

10. Cookies

This site uses only what is necessary to make it work, including remembering your language choice. If we introduce analytics or advertising cookies, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

11. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our processing changes. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
