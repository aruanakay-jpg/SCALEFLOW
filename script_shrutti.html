SCALEFLOW · DISCOVERY SCRIPT · 20 MIN

Ask. Don't pitch.

No price on this call. The offer goes out after, built on her answers.

01 · OPEN 30 SEC
"This isn't a pitch — I don't have a proposal yet. Today I ask questions, show you what we do, and send you an offer built on your answers after. Fair?"
Wait for the yes.
02 · RECAP 1 MIN
"What I took from last time: it's for you alone, you work internationally, lead flow moves with CPL and the feed, you're starting fresh. Correct me anywhere. Fresh is the best starting point — nothing to unpick."
Pause. Let her correct something.
03–06 · QUESTIONS 10 MIN — SHE TALKS

"Where do your buyers come from — Europe, CIS, India, GCC? That decides which languages the system speaks and which hours it stays awake."

"Give me a CPL range and a budget bracket. Honestly — without those, anything I quote is invented."

"And so I don't waste your time on something that doesn't fit — our builds start from $5,000. Is that in your range, or should I look at something lighter?"

"How fast do you really reply? Minutes, hours, or when you see it?"

The $5k question goes right after her budget answer — same topic, not a separate test. Always offer the way out ("or something lighter"); that's what keeps her talking.
Say nothing after the response-time question. The silence gets the truth.
  • Which platforms drive leads? Who runs campaigns now?
  • Strong month vs quiet month — rough numbers.
  • Where do enquiries land? Any CRM?
  • What do you ask to know someone's serious? — write verbatim
  • Off-plan, secondary, rentals? Where does listing data live?
  • 90 days: more leads, better leads, or better follow-up? — make her pick one
Vague on listings? "Fine, we'll map it in the audit." Move on.
07 · WHO WE ARE 30 SEC
"Dubai, real estate only. Two sides: bringing leads in, and making sure none are wasted. We don't sell AI — we sell the outcome."
Don't linger.
08–09 · WHAT SHE GETS 2 MIN

"Traffic side: you pay less per lead, you never run out of creative to test, your feed brings enquiries not likes, and one screen shows where every dirham went.

Follow-up side: every enquiry answered in seconds, in their language, at their hour — asleep, at a viewing, on a plane. You only speak to people worth speaking to. Nobody gets forgotten. One screen, full picture.

Running one side without the other is why lead flow feels inconsistent."

No product names. Tie back to her answer on response time.
10–11 · CASES 2 MIN
"I'll show two closest to your situation. These are real, but from other people's budgets — I'm not promising you the same. We model yours in the audit."
Time zones hurt her → WhatsApp 24/7 case. CPL swings → Meta restructure. Two, not six.
12 · PROCESS 1 MIN
"Audit first — we map the journey and put a number on what leaks. Then build only what the audit justified. Then optimise weekly. I don't want to sell you something and find out in week three the real problem was elsewhere."
13 · CLOSE 1 MIN
"No decision today. I take your answers and send a proposal built around your case — exact scope and timeline within the budget we just talked about. Within 48 hours. If it's wrong, tell me and I fix it."
Confirm before hanging up: channel to send it · what she still owes you (budget, listing source, CPL) · date to speak again.
OBJECTIONS
"How much?"
"Builds start from $5,000 — where you land depends on markets, languages, and whether you want traffic, follow-up, or both. The proposal gives you the exact figure."
"Too early for me."
"Fresh is the cheapest moment. Everyone else pays us to undo three years of leads scattered across WhatsApp."
"I don't know my numbers."
"Give me the roughest version. Where you genuinely don't know — that's what the audit week measures."
"Will AI sound robotic to my clients?"
"Trained on how you speak, you approve every message, anything it can't handle comes to you. The alternative isn't a perfect reply — it's a reply six hours later."
"Let me think."
"Nothing to decide yet. Let me send the proposal first — then you've got something concrete."
FOR THE OFFER
  • Markets + languages, verbatim
  • CPL range + budget bracket, and her reaction to the $5k floor
  • Her honest response time — the whole offer rests on this
  • Her exact qualification questions
  • The one 90-day priority she picked — becomes the headline
Send within 48h. Open by quoting her own words back.
