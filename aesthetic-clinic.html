Common questions

Is this another AI chatbot?

No. Our focus is the workflow: identifying lost opportunities, running approved conversations, booking where possible, and handing off to your team. Not a chatbot interface.

Will this replace my team?

No. The goal is to automate repetitive follow-up and admin work so your team can focus on patients and conversations requiring human attention.

Will the AI provide medical advice?

No. The system is designed for approved administrative and commercial workflows only. Clinical questions are always escalated to your team.

How quickly can we launch?

Five days from approval to going live. After the Revenue Audit, we give you a clear implementation plan before activation.

Does it work with our current CRM?

Likely. Integration depends on your current software and the access it provides. We confirm this during the Revenue Audit before you subscribe.
