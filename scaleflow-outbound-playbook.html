05 / 09
Channel 01 — LinkedIn

Connect. Ask. Listen.

01
Send the connection request
No note, no pitch. A pitch on the request is a declined request.
02
Once accepted — open with a question, not an offer
Hi [Name], thanks for connecting. I came across your agency and wanted to ask you something regarding your sales process.
Then wait. Silence is part of the message.
03
No reply after 2–3 days — get specific
Quick question. How quickly does your team usually respond to Property Finder, Bayut or Meta Ads leads?
Your objective is to understand their process, not to sell it something.
06 / 09
Channel 02 — Instagram

Be the lead before you're the vendor.

01
Message as a genuine buyer
Hi! Is this property still available?
Note the timestamp. Their response time is the evidence you'll use tomorrow.
02
Next day — transition honestly
By the way, I'm not actually looking to purchase. We help Dubai real estate agencies reduce lost commissions caused by delayed responses and missed follow-ups. I thought this might be relevant for your agency. Would you be open to a complimentary 15-minute Executive Revenue Audit?

Why this works

You are not describing a problem to an owner. You have already lived it inside their own funnel, and you have the reply time to prove it. That's not a pitch — that's a data point they can't argue with.

07 / 09
Channel 03 — Cold Calls

Under five minutes.

The call is not the sale. Ask a few questions, find the interest, put the audit in the calendar, hang up. Every extra minute past five moves you from setter to salesperson — and that is the closer's job, not yours.

Call outcome
Booked, or not booked. There is no third result.
Channel 04 — Email
Subject: Quick question about your agency
Hi [Name],

I noticed your agency has a strong presence on Property Finder.

I wanted to ask one quick question.

How does your team currently make sure every enquiry receives a fast response, including evenings and weekends?

We help Dubai real estate agencies identify revenue leaks inside their sales process before they spend more on advertising.

Would you be open to a complimentary Executive Revenue Audit?
Short. Personalised. One question. Never a brochure.

The Cadence

Most audits are booked on the fourth touch.

DAY 01
Initial outreach
DAY 03
Short follow-up question
DAY 06
Relevant insight or case study
DAY 10
Follow-up
DAY 15
Another useful resource
DAY 20
Final follow-up
Never pressure. Every touch gives something — a question, an insight, a resource. Twenty days of usefulness, then you let go.
