QUESTIONS WE'LL CLOSE IN THE FIRST WEEKS

Which CRM exactly? "There's a CRM" isn't enough — we need to know whether it connects to AI automatically.
What WhatsApp is on the work phones? AI can only connect to WhatsApp Business. If it's the ordinary app today, that's 2–4 weeks — which is why we file on day one.
Where will 100 enquiries a day come from? Is 3,000 inbound a month a plan, or does the source already exist?
How does outbound work today? It's your main channel and we have no data on it: where the numbers come from, who calls, what the team in India does.
How do you plan to grow to 10,000 agents? Do agents find their own clients, or do you hand them enquiries centrally? Two completely different products — and it decides which upgrades are worth adding later.
Is Arabic needed? The AI is confident in English and Russian. Arabic in Dubai means several dialects and constant code-switching inside one sentence — materially harder and quoted separately.
Budget and target timeline? Not discussed. We need them to propose a realistic scope rather than guess.
