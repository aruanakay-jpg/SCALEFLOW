Before you ask

Questions owners ask first.

How fast can we go live?+

Package 01 typically deploys in 10–14 days. We connect your portals and WhatsApp, set the qualification logic to your criteria, and start measuring your baseline immediately.

Are you replacing my agents?+

No — and we won't claim we can. The system handles what your agents can't: instant replies, after-hours qualification, and routine paperwork. Your closers get warmer, pre-qualified leads and spend their hours closing.

Which CRM do you work with?+

Propertybase, Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho or your in-house system. Lead cards arrive fully tagged with budget, readiness, timeline and nationality, plus the full chat history.

What is the Revenue Leak Audit, exactly?+

We map your funnel end to end, measure your real baseline — response times, drop-off, follow-up gaps, attribution — and put a dirham figure on each leak. You get the findings and the roadmap whether or not you build with us.

Is my lead data safe?+

Yes. Isolated environments, bank-grade encryption, and no training on your database for shared use. The architecture is built for UAE Data Protection Law, Ejari and goAML standards.

What does it cost?+

Package 01 runs as a fixed-fee pilot, with your baseline measured before it and after it. Everything beyond that is priced stage by stage, with the expected return stated next to it, and part of the fee is tied to the outcome we commit to. The audit puts the numbers on the table before you decide anything.

Who is this not for?+

Agencies looking for a cheap chatbot, and agencies whose lead volume is still too low for the leak to cost real money. If the audit shows the return doesn't justify the build, we say so — that happens, and it saves both sides a quarter.
