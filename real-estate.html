Before you ask

The questions agency owners
ask us first.

How fast can we go live?+

Most agencies are live within 14 days on the core lead responder — often sooner. If we miss 14 days, we keep working for free until it's live. Larger platform builds (voice, Doc-AI) are scoped on their own timeline after the audit.

Will it sound robotic to my buyers?+

No. It holds a natural conversation in Arabic, English and Russian, matches your agency's tone, and hands over to a human agent the moment a lead is qualified or asks for one. Buyers feel attended to, not processed.

Does it work with our CRM and portals?+

Yes — Bayut, Property Finder, WhatsApp Business API, Instagram and most Dubai CRMs (PropSpace and others) connect into one lead stream. If you don't have a CRM yet, we set one up as part of the build.

Is it compliant with Dubai regulations?+

Compliance is built into the workflow: Trakheesi permit gating before any listing publishes, KYC/AML pre-fill with a licensed MLRO signing off, and human review on every Form F / Ejari pack. Automation handles the speed; a licensed human keeps the final signature.

What does it actually cost to run?+

One setup fee to build it, then a monthly retainer for support. Usage costs (Meta WhatsApp fees, LLM tokens, hosting) are billed at cost, separately and transparently — never marked up and hidden. You see the full picture in the audit.

What if it doesn't work for us?+

We baseline your numbers before launch and measure the same metrics after — on your own leads, not a slide. The free audit shows you exactly where leads are leaking before you commit to anything.
