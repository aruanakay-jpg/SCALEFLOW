The call,
slide by slide.

Walkthrough for the 14-slide deck. Speak it, don't read it. Your one job on this call is a yes to Step 1 — the START package.

How to use this. Each block matches one slide. The pause tags are where you stop talking. This client has no CRM and works by phone — so the whole story is "put everything in one place first, then grow." Don't sell AI. Don't sell ads. Sell the one clear system.

SLIDE 1/ 14 · Cover
Frame it as a system, not a tool
"Maruti, thanks for the time. What I've put together isn't an AI pitch — it's a plan to put every enquiry your team gets into one clear system. Everything here came from what you told me. Stop me anywhere."
SLIDE 2/ 14 · What we understood
Set the priority order
"The first priority isn't more advertising, and it isn't a complex AI. pause It's putting every enquiry in one place — so nothing gets lost before you grow. Make it visible first, then advertise, then automate."
SLIDE 3/ 14 · Problem 01 — scattered
Name what he lives with daily
"Right now enquiries are scattered — calls, buyer requirements, next actions, all in different places. pause The fix is simple: one place where every call and requirement lands in the same list."
SLIDE 4/ 14 · Problem 02 — held in memory
The risk of a team of 10
"Every next step is held in memory — there's no list of who contacts which client, and when. pause The fix: one responsible person per enquiry, with the next step recorded, not remembered."
SLIDE 5/ 14 · Problem 03 — manager can't see
Speak to the owner directly
"As the manager, you can't easily see workload, reply speed, or which enquiries are actually moving toward a deal. pause The fix: one report — workload, reply speed and status, on one screen."
SLIDE 6/ 14 · Problem 04 — ads add confusion
Protect him from wasting money
"And here's the important one: spending on ads before the process is fixed just brings enquiries nobody handles properly. pause Fix the process first — then advertising brings leads into a system that can actually hold them."
SLIDE 7/ 14 · What we suggest
The honest sequence
"So what we suggest is an order, not a big spend. pause First, one place for every enquiry. Then test advertising. Then add automatic steps only where they're clearly needed. One list, one owner, one report."
SLIDE 8/ 14 · What we do first
The START package, concretely
"Step one is the START package: a CRM set up around your work, the channels connected, one owner per enquiry, WhatsApp replies and reminders, a manager's report, plus training and handover. pause Nothing lives in memory — every opportunity is visible from day one."
SLIDE 9/ 14 · What we'll check
We invent no percentages
"I won't promise you a number of enquiries before we've measured anything. pause At the start we record the real figures — are all enquiries saved, how fast you reply, do next contacts happen, where clients stop — then we agree together what should improve."
SLIDE 10/ 14 · The three steps
Show the whole road
"Three steps. One: one place for enquiries — that's what we do now. Two: replies and reminders, once we check your phone and WhatsApp setup — included in START. Three: only after step one, we test one or two ad channels with a separate budget. pause We never skip to step three."
SLIDE 11/ 14 · Three packages
Anchor START before any price
"The build comes in three sizes. pause The middle one, START, is what I recommend — it's the full 'one place for every enquiry.' Entry is the lightest way in; Growth adds a real AI assistant and the ad test. You choose the level; we confirm scope after a short channel review."
SLIDE 12/ 14 · A few questions
What makes the price exact
"To quote you exactly I need a few things: which phone numbers and WhatsApp version the team uses, where enquiries come from today and whether there's a contact database, and which segments are priority. pause Answer those and I can lock the exact scope."
SLIDE 13/ 14 · Prices — only now
Let the slide land before you speak
pause — let him read the whole slide first "Entry from $2,250. START — the one I recommend — from $4,800. Growth from $8,700. Support on top, discount already in. Third-party costs — CRM licences, WhatsApp, AI usage, ad budget — go straight to the provider, zero markup. Payment: half to start, half on completion."
Silence after the START number is your friend. Don't rush.
SLIDE 14/ 14 · What's in each
Everything builds on the last
"Last slide — the three side by side, so you see what steps up between them. pause Each builds on the one before, so if you start at Entry you're never rebuilding to reach START later."
THE CLOSE · AFTER THE DECK
AFTER SLIDE 14· The close

Ask for Step 1 — nothing bigger.

"So that's the whole picture, Maruti. I'm not asking you to commit to ads or a big system today — just Step 1, the one place for every enquiry. We do a short review of your channels, I confirm the exact scope and price, and you decide with real numbers. pause — then ask, and stop talking Can we start that this week?"
Do not fill the silence. The next person to speak should be Maruti.
OBJECTION QUICK-REFERENCE
✕ "I want more leads first — can't you just run ads?"
"I can, but I'd be spending your money badly. Ads before the system just bring enquiries nobody handles — you'd pay for leads that still get lost. One place first, then ads land somewhere that holds them. That's why Step 1 comes before Step 3."
✕ "It's expensive for a small team."
"That's exactly why START is sized for about 10 people — it's the one place for every enquiry, nothing more. Entry is lighter still if you want to start smaller. And the price is confirmed after a short review, so there are no surprises."
✕ "We don't even have a CRM — is this too early?"
"It's the opposite — no CRM is exactly why now is the right time. We set one up around how you already work, so nothing lives in memory. You're not fixing an old system; you're starting clean."
✕ "Can you guarantee more deals?"
"No — and I won't invent a percentage before I've seen your numbers. That's why we measure at the start. What I guarantee is that every enquiry becomes visible and nothing gets lost. The growth conversation comes after, on real figures."
✕ "Let me think about it."
"Of course. The thinking's easier once we've done the short channel review — it costs you nothing and gives you the exact scope and price. Shall we at least start there?"
✕ "Send me the details."
"I will — I've got the written proposal ready to send right after this. The prices are set; the review just confirms the exact scope. Can we get it booked while I've got you?"
