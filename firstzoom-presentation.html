LEAK 01 · SPEED

Real-estate agency · Dubai

150–200 inbound leads / mo · 4 agents
Before

"We're paying Bayut and Property Finder every month. The leads are coming in. So why are we closing the same number of deals as last year?"

What the owner said on our first call

The answer wasn't in the traffic. It was in the four hours between an enquiry landing and someone seeing it. Four agents, 150–200 leads a month — they physically worked whichever lead happened to be on top. A buyer enquiring at 8pm got a reply the next morning. By then he'd already spoken to three other agencies and booked a viewing with one of them.

Nobody was lazy. Each agent burned up to 3 hours a day copy-pasting enquiries into the CRM and writing the same follow-up for the tenth time. The most expensive people in the business were doing data entry while ready buyers went cold.

AED 180,000+ a year in commission on leads they'd already paid for and never reached in time
What we built
  • Every lead from Bayut, Property Finder and the website auto-syncs into the CRM — manual entry removed entirely
  • First reply on WhatsApp inside 90 seconds, referencing the specific property, price range and the buyer's name
  • Intent scoring: hot leads pushed to agents instantly, warm leads into a 7-day nurture sequence
  • Self-service viewing scheduler synced to agent calendars, with reminders that cut no-shows
What was actually broken: not the traffic, not the agents — the gap between an enquiry landing and a human seeing it. Buying more leads would have widened that gap, not closed it.

LEAK 02 · FOLLOW-UP

Mid-size agency · 15–20 brokers

Bayut + Property Finder · 3,000+ dormant contacts
Before

"I'm spending six figures a year on portals. Ask me which one actually brings deals and I can't tell you. I'm guessing."

What the owner said on our first call

Money went out across Bayut and Property Finder every month, and nobody could say what came back. Not because the team was careless — because the answer lived in four places at once and nowhere in full. Budget decisions were made on instinct.

Meanwhile the best brokers — the ones who actually close — spent up to 60% of their day filtering spam and answering "just asking the off-plan price." And 3,000 people who had already viewed a property and simply hadn't bought yet sat in the database untouched. Every one of them had raised a hand once. Nobody ever got around to calling back.

3,000 buyers already paid for, sitting in the CRM — while new budget was spent to find replacements for them
What we built
  • Deep CRM integration that doesn't just chat — it distributes deals by district, tag and broker specialisation
  • Bayut, Property Finder and WhatsApp merged into one workspace, every lead carrying its source tag
  • Automated nurture across the sleeping base: touch chains on new inventory and price changes, strictly opt-in
  • One owner view: cost per valid lead and per closed deal, by portal — not cost per click
What was actually broken: they were buying new leads to replace the ones they already had and never called. The database wasn't dead — it was unopened.

LEAK 03 · CAPACITY

Large broker / developer

10,000+ enquiries a month · call centre + back office
Before

"Every time volume goes up, I hire. Revenue grows, profit doesn't. I'm running faster to stand still."

What the owner said on our first call

A large call centre and a full back office — and the economics still didn't work. At peak hours calls went unanswered. Not occasionally: predictably, every single day, because ten thousand enquiries a month don't arrive politely spaced out.

The real damage was at the finish line. Preparing Form F and Ejari, pre-filling RERA and DLD paperwork took 2–3 hours of manual re-typing. That's 2–3 hours a buyer sits waiting at the exact moment he is most certain — and most likely to be pulled away by an agency that can move today. Deals were being lost after the client had already said yes.

Every AED of growth converted straight into payroll. The only lever available was hiring — so margin compressed while revenue climbed
What we built
  • Every inbound channel — WhatsApp, web, phone, email — answered in one flow, at any hour, without a night shift
  • New enquiries reached within seconds, in Arabic or English, with viewing slots booked automatically
  • Passports, Emirates ID and title deeds read automatically; Form F and Ejari arrive pre-filled — the manager approves a finished draft instead of building it by hand
  • Judgement stays with people. The repetition doesn't
What was actually broken: growth was tied to payroll. Every extra lead needed an extra human — so scale was something the owner bought, not something the business produced.
