What we heard

All of this came from your own answers on the call.

The first reply is fast — then silence. A warm buyer waits 7–10 days for the next contact, and in that window speaks to several competing agents. You said it yourself: the follow-up is where it slips.
7–10 days is a blind zone. That's long enough for a buyer to view a property, get an offer, and commit — all with the agent who kept messaging. By the time your agent follows up, the conversation is already over somewhere else.
Across 30 agents, the next contact depends on memory. Without one shared list of tasks, whether a lead is followed up comes down to which agent happened to remember — 30 people, 30 private systems, no single source of truth.
When an agent leaves, his enquiries leave too. Commission pays for closing, not for logging — so good leads sit on a personal phone until they're worth logging, and some never are. That's business walking out the door.
Many enquiries arrive after the office closes. A large share of Dubai property interest comes in evenings and weekends. A 20-minute daytime reply quietly becomes all night on a Saturday — and first-to-answer usually wins.
You can't yet see where deals stop. ~2–3 deals per 100 enquiries — but nobody can prove whether that's the market, the advertising, or the follow-up. You're renewing every portal on faith.
A slow market isn't the only problem. We can't change the market — but a forgotten enquiry hurts most exactly when leads are scarce. The ones you already paid for become worth more, not less.
Your first response is already fast. 20–30 minutes, sometimes instant — better than most Dubai brokerages. The start isn't the problem, so we won't rebuild it.
Your agents already work company calls. We keep that strategy — no cold calling, no voice robot forced on the team. We add to what works, we don't replace it.

What the gap
actually costs

The leak is invisible on your books — which is exactly why it keeps happening.

You paid for the enquiry twice
Once to generate it through ads and portals — then again in the agent hours spent on a first reply that never gets a second. The spend is real; the return leaks away in the silence.
Every day of silence is a competitor's opening
The buyer didn't wait for you. In 7–10 days he had three or four other conversations. Whoever stayed in touch earned the viewing — often on a property you could have shown first.
The cost hides inside "the market is slow"
It's easy to blame the market for 2–3 deals per 100. But part of that number is follow-up that never happened — and that part is entirely inside your control.
It compounds across 30 agents
One forgotten follow-up is a rounding error. Thirty agents, each forgetting a few a week, is a pipeline quietly bleeding every month — with no report to show where.

What we do about it

We don't replace your team or your strategy. We make sure nothing gets forgotten — four moving parts.

Capture — every enquiry, every channel, in one place. WhatsApp, web, portals and forms all land in one flow with their source and time. Nothing sits in a personal inbox, nothing waits for someone to notice it — including at 11pm on a Saturday, when the AI answers instantly and qualifies the lead.
Assign — one responsible agent on every enquiry. The moment a lead arrives it has a named owner. No more "I thought you had it." The manager can see, per enquiry, exactly who is accountable.
Schedule — a next-contact date and action on every lead. This is the piece that kills the 7–10 day gap. Every enquiry carries a date and a required next step, and the system reminds the agent automatically. Follow-up stops depending on memory and starts happening on time, across all 30 agents.
Report — one live board for the whole team. Response speed, next-contact compliance, conversion by source and by agent — on one screen, without anyone typing. You finally see whether it's the market, the ads, or the follow-up.
