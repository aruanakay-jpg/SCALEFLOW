QUESTIONS · 01 · MARKETS & LANGUAGE

Who are you
actually selling to?

  1. Which countries do your buyers come from?This decides which languages the system speaks and which hours it needs to stay awake.
  2. Are you selling Dubai property abroad, or property abroad from Dubai?The direction changes the whole funnel.
  3. Buyers, sellers, tenants — or a mix?Each needs a different qualification path.
QUESTIONS · 02 · CPL & LEAD FLOW

Where does the
traffic come from?

  1. Which platforms drive leads today — Meta, Google, Instagram organic, referrals?And who runs the campaigns: you, an agency, or nobody yet?
  2. What's your CPL now, and what would a good CPL be?You mentioned it swings. We want the range, not a perfect number.
  3. What's your monthly ad budget?Even a bracket is enough to size the build correctly.
  4. Strong month vs quiet month — how many enquiries?Rough figures are fine. We're looking for the shape, not the spreadsheet.
QUESTIONS · 03 · WHAT HAPPENS AFTER A LEAD LANDS

The part where
money leaks.

  1. Where do enquiries arrive — WhatsApp, DMs, email, a form?And are they in one place or four?
  2. Honestly, how fast do you reply?Minutes, hours, or "when I see it" — no wrong answer.
  3. Do you use a CRM, or is it chat history and memory?Starting fresh means we can build this properly the first time.
  4. What do you ask to decide if a lead is serious?We'll teach the system your qualification, not a generic one.
QUESTIONS · 04 · INVENTORY & OUTCOME

What you sell,
and what winning
looks like.

  1. Off-plan, secondary, rentals — and how many active listings?You weren't sure on listing times; that's fine, we'll map it together.
  2. Where does property data live — portal, spreadsheet, developer feed?The system needs one source of truth to answer buyer questions.
  3. In 90 days: more leads, better leads, or better follow-up?Pick one to lead with. We'll build toward it.
  4. Timeline and budget range?So the proposal we send is real, not aspirational.
  5. Our builds start from $5,000 — is that the right range for you?Asked early on purpose. Better to find out now than after three calls.
