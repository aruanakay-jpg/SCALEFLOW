PHASE 01Week 1–2 · Revenue Foundation

Every enquiry lands in one place.

We stop the leak before we automate anything. One system, one list, one owner per client.

  • CRM selected and configured around how your team actually sells
  • Pipeline stages, fields and access set up for all ~10 agents
  • WhatsApp capture live — every enquiry recorded before an agent touches it
  • Routing rules: one named owner on every enquiry, assigned automatically
  • Instant notification to the assigned agent
  • Existing contacts imported and cleaned
What you can see at the endOpen the CRM and every live enquiry in the agency is on one screen, with a name against it and a next step with a date.
PHASE 02Week 2–3 · Revenue Automation

The routine runs without anyone remembering it.

Now that enquiries are captured, we take the repeatable work off the team.

  • AI qualification on WhatsApp — purpose, budget, area, timeline, in EN/RU
  • Instant first reply, day or night, logged to the record
  • Automatic follow-up so contact two, three and four actually happen
  • Viewing booking straight into the agent's calendar
  • Reminders 24h and 2h before, to client and agent
  • Escalation if an assigned enquiry sits untouched
What you can see at the endA new enquiry at 11pm gets a reply in seconds, arrives qualified in the morning, and a viewing is already on the calendar.
PHASE 03Week 4 · Revenue Intelligence

You can finally see the business.

The system now holds the truth about your pipeline. This turns it into the screen you run Monday mornings from.

  • Owner's dashboard: reply time, open enquiries, overdue tasks, pipeline by stage
  • Enquiries by source and by agent, so ad spend is judged on outcome
  • Weekly report delivered automatically to you, not requested from the team
  • Baseline figures recorded, and targets agreed from them
  • Team training, written operating rules, handover
What you can see at the endFour numbers that tell you whether last week was good, before anyone tells you it was.

Straight answers

What this roadmap does not promise.

It doesn't generate new enquiries

This converts the enquiries you already get. Finding new ones is a separate second step, taken once this is running.

It doesn't guarantee a number of deals

We record your real figures in week one and agree targets from them. Any percentage quoted before that measurement would be invented.

Some scope depends on a technical check

Your phone numbers and WhatsApp version decide what can be connected officially. That check happens in week one.

It only works if the team uses it

Training and written rules are included, and the dashboard shows you within days whether adoption is real.
