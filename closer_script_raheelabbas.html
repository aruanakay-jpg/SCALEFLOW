Closer Script · ScaleFlow × Raheel Abbas

The call,
slide by slide.

Walkthrough for the 19-slide deck. Speak it, don't read it. Your one job on this call is a yes to the audit.

How to use this. Each block below matches one slide. The pause tags are where you stop talking and let it land — they do more work than any sentence. Everything before the price slides makes the 7–10 day gap feel real and expensive. Everything after makes the decision feel safe. Don't sell the package. Sell the audit.

SLIDE 1/ 19 · Cover
Frame it as an audit, not a pitch
"Raheel, thanks for the time. This isn't a pitch deck — it's an audit of where your enquiries actually go across your 30 agents. Everything in here came from what you told me. Stop me anywhere."
SLIDE 2/ 19 · Every enquiry already cost you money
One idea: you already paid for every lead
"Every enquiry — you already paid for it. Ads, portals, your team's time. pause So the only question is: out of 100 enquiries, how many do 30 agents turn into deals? Right now, two or three. The other 97 were paid for too. This is about where they went."
SLIDE 3/ 19 · Six ways an enquiry dies
The emotional core — slow down
"Six ways a paid enquiry quietly dies in a team your size. pause — let him scan Fast first reply, then silence for 7–10 days while they message other agencies. The next contact depends on whoever remembered. pause None of these is a lead problem — it's a leak between the enquiry and the deal."
Let him recognize his own business here.
SLIDE 4/ 19 · Your first reply is already fast
Be fair to his team — buys credibility
"To be fair to your team: your first reply is genuinely fast — 20-30 minutes, better than most in Dubai. pause So the start isn't your problem, and I'm not here to rebuild it. The leak is the silence after it."
SLIDE 5/ 19 · The 7–10 day gap
The gap, by the day
"Day one, agent replies, buyer's interested. Day two to ten — nothing. Meanwhile he's viewing with the agent who kept messaging. Day ten you follow up — too late. pause Same lead, same price — you paid to be second. The fix: every enquiry gets a next-contact date the moment it lands, and the system reminds the agent, not his memory."
SLIDE 6/ 19 · 30 agents, 30 systems
The problem multiplies by thirty
"One agent's follow-up is in his head, another's in a notebook, a third's in a personal WhatsApp. No single list of who to contact today. pause The fix: one shared list, same rules for all thirty — one owner, one next step, one live board for you."
SLIDE 7/ 19 · When an agent leaves
Leads walk out the door
"Commission pays for closing, not logging — so good leads sit on a personal phone. When that agent leaves, they leave with him. pause The fix: the enquiry lands in your system before the agent ever sees it. Your database stays yours."
SLIDE 8/ 19 · Nights
Buyers write after the office closes
"A lot of these come in at night and on weekends. Your 20-minute daytime reply becomes all night on a Saturday. pause The AI doesn't sleep — every enquiry gets an instant, qualified reply, and your agent wakes up to a warm conversation."
SLIDE 9/ 19 · The market
Pre-empt his biggest objection
"You said the market's slow — you're right, and I won't pretend to change it. pause But a slow market is when a forgotten enquiry hurts most. I'm not promising more leads — I'm making sure the ones you paid for don't vanish in that gap."
SLIDE 10/ 19 · Money in the pipeline
Revive what's already there
"Before we chase a new lead — there's money sitting in your pipeline now. Enquiries that went quiet, never got a second message. pause Follow-up runs back through them automatically, by segment. The ready ones reply — zero extra agent hours. This pays off in week one."
SLIDE 11/ 19 · The dashboard
You can't see where deals stop
"You get 2–3 per 100 — but nobody can prove why the other 97 didn't close. You can't see response times, which source brings deals, or which agent converts. pause The fix: one screen, every enquiry from first message to signed deal. Finally you know if it's the market, the ads, or the follow-up."
SLIDE 12/ 19 · What we don't do
Remove the fear before the solution
"What we're NOT doing: no replacing your team, no cold calling, no robot on your agents' phones. Your calls and strategy stay. pause We remove work, we don't add it. Four things: capture, assign, schedule, report."
SLIDE 13/ 19 · What we capture
Proof it's concrete — keep it quick
"This stops living in agents' heads: source and time, real response time, one owner, the next-contact date, viewing results, why a deal closed or died. pause Once it's in place, you stop guessing."
SLIDE 14/ 19 · Three ways forward
Anchor Growth before any price
"Three ways forward, each builds on the last. pause The middle one, Growth, is what I'd recommend — it closes the 7–10 day gap we've spent this call on. One is the lighter floor, three automates the whole funnel. You choose the level; we confirm exact scope after the audit."
SLIDE 15/ 19 · How we de-risk
Make the decision feel safe
"We don't build before we check your numbers. A short audit, then a fixed scope and price. pause I won't promise '+40% deals' before seeing your data — that's vendor marketing. The audit doesn't commit you to the build."
SLIDE 16/ 19 · What the audit checks
Concrete, five things
"The audit checks five things: true first-reply time, whether next contacts happen on time, how each agent follows the plan, where clients stop, and results by source and agent. pause And we verify how that 2–3 per 100 was actually counted before agreeing any target."
SLIDE 17/ 19 · Three questions
What makes the price exact
"To quote you exactly I need three things: which CRM and telephony you use, how many enquiries a month and from where, and how the 2–3 per 100 was counted. pause Answer those and the price stops being a 'from'."
SLIDE 18/ 19 · Prices — only now
Let the slide land before you speak
pause — let him read the whole slide first "Foundation from $3,750. Growth — the one I recommend — from $15,900. Enterprise from $44,400. Support on top, discount already in. Third-party costs go straight to the provider, zero markup. Payment: half to start, half on completion."
Silence after the Growth number is your friend. Don't rush.
SLIDE 19/ 19 · What's in each
Everything builds on the last
"Same three side by side, so you see what steps up between them. pause Each builds on the one before — start at Foundation and you're never rebuilding to reach Growth later."
THE CLOSE · AFTER THE DECK
AFTER SLIDE 19· The close

Ask for the audit — nothing bigger.

"So that's the whole picture, Raheel. The only thing I'm asking today isn't a decision on a package — just the audit. We check your real numbers, I come back with exact scope and price, you decide with data in front of you. pause — then ask, and stop talking Can we run that audit this week?"
Do not fill the silence. The next person to speak should be Raheel.
OBJECTION QUICK-REFERENCE
✕ "It's expensive."
"Compared to what? One extra deal a month covers the Growth setup in a single cycle. Right now you're losing warm buyers to competitors inside that 7–10 day gap — that's the real cost, and it's invisible on your books today. The audit shows you exactly how big it is."
✕ "The market's slow — bad time."
"That's the strongest reason to do it, not to wait. We're not promising more leads. We're making sure the ones you already paid for don't get forgotten. In a slow market that's worth more, not less."
✕ "My agents won't use it."
"That's exactly why there's no cold calling and no robot. Their job doesn't change — the system just tells them who to contact today. It removes admin, it doesn't add any. And training all 30 is included in Growth."
✕ "Can you guarantee more deals?"
"No — and I won't pretend to before I've seen your numbers. That's what the audit is for. What I guarantee is that no enquiry gets forgotten and you'll finally see where deals stop. Everything after that is built on your real figures."
✕ "Let me think about it."
"Of course — and the thinking's easier with real data. The audit doesn't lock you into the build; it just gives you the exact scope and price. Shall we at least start there?"
✕ "Send me the details."
"I will — I've got a written proposal ready to send right after this. Take your time with it. But the numbers in it stay 'from' until the audit; that's the one step that makes them exact. Can we get it booked while I've got you?"
