The call,
slide by slide.

Walkthrough for the 19-slide deck. Speak it, don't read it. Your one job on this call is a yes to the audit.

How to use this. Each block below matches one slide. The pause tags are where you stop talking and let it land — they do more work than any sentence. Everything before the price slides makes the 7–10 day gap feel real and expensive. Everything after makes the decision feel safe. Don't sell the package. Sell the audit.

SLIDE 1/ 14 · Cover
Frame it as an audit, not a pitch
"Raheel, thanks for the time. This isn't a pitch deck — it's an audit of where your enquiries go across your 30 agents. Everything in here came from your own answers. Stop me anywhere."
SLIDE 2/ 14 · What we heard
Reflect his own numbers back
"Quick recap of what you told me: agents perform well, first response is fast — 20-30 minutes. Next contact comes after 7-10 days. About 2-3 deals per 100 enquiries. pause The first response is already fast. The real risk is what comes next."
SLIDE 3/ 14 · Problem 01 — first response isn't the gap
Be fair to his team — buys credibility
"To be fair to your team, your first reply is genuinely fast — better than most in Dubai. pause A 20-30 minute reply can still be improved, but the bigger risk is the silence that follows it. That's the real leak."
SLIDE 4/ 14 · Problem 02 — the 7-10 day blind zone
The gap that costs deals
"Here's the blind zone: 7 to 10 days before the next contact. That's long enough for a buyer to speak with several competing agents while yours is still waiting. pause The fix: every enquiry gets a next-contact date and action — so it happens on time, not from memory."
SLIDE 5/ 14 · Problem 03 — 30 agents, same rules
The problem multiplies by thirty
"Without one shared list of tasks, the next contact depends on each agent's memory — and you've got thirty of them. pause The fix: the same simple rules for everyone, one responsible agent per enquiry, one clear report for you."
SLIDE 6/ 14 · Problem 04 — the slow market
Pre-empt his biggest objection
"You mentioned the market's slow — you're right, and we can't change it. pause But a slow market isn't the whole story. We can stop enquiries from being forgotten and make responsibility clear — that part is fully in your control."
SLIDE 7/ 14 · What we suggest
We keep what works
"So what we suggest isn't a new strategy. We don't replace your team with cold calling — we keep your company calls. pause We just save every result in one place and show every next contact. That's the whole idea."
SLIDE 8/ 14 · What we save per enquiry
Proof it's concrete — keep it quick
"For every enquiry we save the same things: source and time, actual response time, one responsible agent, the next-contact date, viewing results, and why it closed or was lost. pause Nothing lives in an agent's head anymore."
SLIDE 9/ 14 · What the audit checks
The included first step
"The first 1-2 weeks are an audit, included, no extra fee. We check your true first-reply time, whether next contacts happen on time, how agents follow the plan, where clients stop, and results by source and agent. pause And we verify how that 2-3 per 100 was actually counted before agreeing any target."
SLIDE 10/ 14 · The three steps
How the work runs
"Three steps. One: check the numbers — included. Two: the build — no enquiry forgotten, one owner and a next-contact date on each. Three: after 30 days, we tune the process on your real figures. pause No cold calling at any step."
SLIDE 11/ 14 · Three packages
Anchor Growth before any price
"The build comes in three sizes. pause The middle one, Growth, is what I'd recommend — it's the full answer to the 7-10 day gap. One is the lighter floor, three automates the whole funnel. You choose the level; we confirm exact scope after the audit."
SLIDE 12/ 14 · Three questions
What makes the price exact
"To quote you exactly I need three things: which CRM and telephony you use, how many enquiries a month and from where, and how the 2-3 per 100 was counted. pause Answer those and I lock the exact scope to the price."
SLIDE 13/ 14 · Prices — only now
Let the slide land before you speak
pause — let him read the whole slide first "Foundation, $3,750. Growth — the one I recommend — $15,900. Enterprise, $44,400. Support on top, discount already in. Third-party costs go straight to the provider, zero markup. Payment: half to start, half on completion."
Silence after the Growth number is your friend. Don't rush.
SLIDE 14/ 14 · What's in each
Everything builds on the last
"Last slide — the same three side by side, so you see what steps up between them. pause Each builds on the one before, so if you start at Foundation you're never rebuilding to reach Growth later."
THE CLOSE · AFTER THE DECK
AFTER SLIDE 14· The close

Ask for the audit — nothing bigger.

"So that's the whole picture, Raheel. The only thing I'm asking today isn't a decision on a package — just the audit. We check your real numbers, I come back with exact scope and price, you decide with data in front of you. pause — then ask, and stop talking Can we run that audit this week?"
Do not fill the silence. The next person to speak should be Raheel.
OBJECTION QUICK-REFERENCE
✕ "It's expensive."
"Compared to what? One extra deal a month covers the Growth setup in a single cycle. Right now you're losing warm buyers to competitors inside that 7–10 day gap — that's the real cost, and it's invisible on your books today. The audit shows you exactly how big it is."
✕ "The market's slow — bad time."
"That's the strongest reason to do it, not to wait. We're not promising more leads. We're making sure the ones you already paid for don't get forgotten. In a slow market that's worth more, not less."
✕ "My agents won't use it."
"That's exactly why there's no cold calling and no robot. Their job doesn't change — the system just tells them who to contact today. It removes admin, it doesn't add any. And training all 30 is included in Growth."
✕ "Can you guarantee more deals?"
"No — and I won't pretend to before I've seen your numbers. That's what the audit is for. What I guarantee is that no enquiry gets forgotten and you'll finally see where deals stop. Everything after that is built on your real figures."
✕ "Let me think about it."
"Of course — and the thinking's easier with real data. The audit doesn't lock you into the build; it just gives you the exact scope and price. Shall we at least start there?"
✕ "Send me the details."
"I will — I've got a written proposal ready to send right after this. Take your time with it. The prices are set; the audit just confirms the exact scope against them. Can we get it booked while I've got you?"
