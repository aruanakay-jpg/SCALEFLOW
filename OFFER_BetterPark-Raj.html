How we make

We measure your numbers before we build anything.

  1. Before we build
    We measure your real numbers today — how long an enquiry actually waits, how many reach a viewing, how many become deals. Those go into the contract. We also file the WhatsApp Business application on day one, so its 2–4 week approval runs in parallel instead of delaying the launch.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2 — the audit
    Included in the Stage 1 price. Which CRM you have and whether it connects to AI, what WhatsApp is on the phones, where enquiries come from, how outbound works today. We also put the legal basis for UAE–India data transfer in place. The output is an exact quote that doesn't change afterwards.
  3. Weeks 3 to 8 — build and launch
    We build and launch. Your team gives us CRM access and one hour to agree the questions the AI asks. That's the whole ask.
  4. Then we measure
    Against the numbers we started with. The contract fixes the target: reply time under 5 minutes, down from 2–3 hours. Part of the payment is due only if we hit it. If we don't, we keep working until it does — at our cost, not yours.

We don't promise "+40% more leads." Nobody can know that before seeing your data.
The only number that matters is the one we measure on your own leads.

QUESTIONS WE'LL CLOSE IN THE FIRST WEEKS

Which CRM exactly? "There's a CRM" isn't enough — we need to know whether it connects to AI automatically.
What WhatsApp is on the work phones? AI can only connect to WhatsApp Business. If it's the ordinary app today, that's 2–4 weeks — which is why we file on day one.
Where will 100 enquiries a day come from? Is 3,000 inbound a month a plan, or does the source already exist?
How does outbound work today? It's your main channel and we have no data on it: where the numbers come from, who calls, what the team in India does.
How do you plan to grow to 10,000 agents? Do agents find their own clients, or do you hand them enquiries centrally? Two completely different products — and everything in Stage 3 depends on the answer.
Is Arabic needed? The AI is confident in English and Russian. Arabic in Dubai means several dialects and constant code-switching inside one sentence — materially harder and quoted separately.
Budget and target timeline? Not discussed. We need them to propose a realistic scope rather than guess.
