01

Who we are

Foundation · Why we exist

ScaleFlow is a revenue-recovery partner for Dubai real-estate agencies. We happen to use AI to do it.

Every agency in this city spends heavily to generate leads — Bayut, Property Finder, Meta, portals, referrals. Then a large share of those paid leads die in the gaps: unanswered at night, dropped over the weekend, never followed up, buried in a CRM nobody works. We plug those gaps. Our voice agents, WhatsApp automation, and CRM workflows exist for one purpose: make sure no paid lead is ever lost again.

Say this, not that

Never: "We build AI chatbots and CRM automation." → Always: "We make sure the agents you already paid to acquire actually get called back — so you stop handing commissions to competitors."

The Revenue Leak Audit

The Offer · This is the only thing you sell

Stop selling packages. Sell one thing: a Revenue Leak Audit. Packages come after the owner sees their own numbers.

This is our single strongest idea and the thing almost no competitor does. Instead of pitching "AI automation," you offer to run a diagnostic on where the agency is already losing money. During the audit we inspect every place a paid lead can die:

✓CRM — is anything actually worked?
✓WhatsApp — response time & drop-off
✓Bayut lead handling
✓Property Finder lead handling
✓Meta / paid campaigns → follow-up
✓Speed-to-lead (day, night, weekend)

11

FAQ

Fast answers, always back to revenue
Q1Is the audit really free?+
Yes. You keep the Revenue Leakage Report whether or not you work with us. It costs us time; it costs you nothing but 30 minutes.
Q2Will AI replace my brokers?+
No. It removes the work brokers hate — chasing dead leads and after-hours replies — so they spend time closing warm, qualified buyers instead.
Q3We already have a CRM.+
Good — we work inside it. The problem is rarely the CRM; it's that nobody works the leads in it. We fix the workflow, not the software.
Q4How fast do we see results?+
Speed-to-lead and weekend coverage improve from day one. Reactivation of the old database usually produces booked viewings within the first few weeks.
Q5What does it cost?+
We price against what you recover. After the audit shows the leak, we map a package to it — so the fee is a fraction of the revenue it returns.
Q6We tried automation before, it flopped.+
Most "automation" is a bot bolted on with no process behind it. We start with the audit and fix the process first — the tech only comes after.
12

Objections

Acknowledge · reframe · return to the leak
O1"It's too expensive."+
Compared to what? You're already paying to generate leads and losing a chunk of them. This costs less than the commissions leaking out every weekend. The audit shows you the exact figure.
O2"I need to think about it."+
Completely fair. While you think, the leak keeps running. The audit is free and there's no commitment — let's at least get you the number to think against.
O3"Send me a proposal / email."+
I can, but a generic proposal is useless without your numbers. Give me 30 minutes for the audit and the "proposal" becomes your actual leakage report.
O4"My team can handle this."+
Your team is great at closing — that's why they shouldn't be babysitting a chatbot at midnight. We cover the gaps so they focus on deals.
O5"We're too busy right now."+
Busy is exactly when leads slip. That's the leak. The setup is on us; the audit takes 30 minutes; the payoff frees your time.
O6"Does this even work for real estate?"+
It's built for it. High commissions and speed-sensitive buyers are exactly where recovered response time turns into closed deals. Case studies coming up in module 16.
